About Celes13

Built in studios. Sharpened on the sidelines.

Celes13 — short for Çelës 13, the spanner — is a marketing studio led by Enur Pakashtica, a producer, strategist, and content creator with an MBA in Sports Management from Escuela Universitaria Real Madrid.

Over a decade in front of the camera and behind the strategy, we've built shows seen by millions, scaled brands across Europe, and shot the biggest stages in football and basketball — Champions League, Nations League, FIBA tournaments, and Real Madrid itself.

We don't sell templates. We arrive, diagnose, and tighten the bolt that nobody else could reach. That's why we're number 13.

What we do

From strategy to camera, from social feeds to live broadcasts — Celes13 is a full marketing toolkit, built on real-world experience.

Brand & Marketing Strategy

Photography & Videography

Match days, campaign shoots, behind-the-scenes, brand films. Accredited at Champions League and FIBA level — the work speaks for itself.

TV & Live Production
